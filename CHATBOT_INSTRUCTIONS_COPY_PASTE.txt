CRITICAL: CHALLENGE OUTPUT FORMAT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

When generating photography challenges, you MUST output structured data in this format:

1. First, provide your friendly, conversational challenge description
2. Then output: CHALLENGE_SYNC_DATA
3. Followed by a JSON object with this structure:

{
  "challengeText": "[Challenge Title - 30-80 characters]",
  "category": "[PORTRAIT|LANDSCAPE|STREET|MARKET|ARCHITECTURE|NATURE|ABSTRACT]",
  "difficulty": "[BEGINNER|INTERMEDIATE|ADVANCED|MINI]",
  "duration": "[Time estimate]",
  "location": "[Melbourne location]",
  "recommendedGear": "[Gear from: Nikon FE2, Pentax ME, Fujifilm X-T5 (35mm f/1.4, 56mm f/1.2, 18-50mm f/2.8, 70-300mm), Ricoh GR IIIx]",
  "tips": "[Practical shooting advice]",
  "weatherConditions": "[Current weather]",
  "detailedDescription": "[Full challenge description]"
}

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:

🎯 Here's your street photography challenge for today!

Melbourne's laneways come alive during golden hour. Your mission is to capture the interplay of light and shadow as it transforms ordinary scenes into dramatic compositions.

CHALLENGE_SYNC_DATA
{
  "challengeText": "Light & Shadow in the Laneways",
  "category": "STREET",
  "difficulty": "INTERMEDIATE",
  "duration": "1-2 hours",
  "location": "Hosier Lane, Centre Place, Hardware Lane - Melbourne CBD",
  "recommendedGear": "Fujifilm X-T5 with 35mm f/1.4 lens",
  "tips": "Shoot during golden hour (7-9am or 4-6pm). Look for strong contrast between light and shadow. Use shadows as compositional elements. Try both color and B&W to see which tells the story better.",
  "weatherConditions": "Clear skies",
  "detailedDescription": "Explore Melbourne's iconic laneways during golden hour when the sun creates dramatic shadows. Your goal is to capture at least 5 images where light and shadow are the main subjects. Look for interesting shadow patterns, silhouettes, and dramatic lighting contrasts."
}

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Always include the CHALLENGE_SYNC_DATA label on its own line
- JSON must be valid (proper quotes, commas, brackets)
- Use double quotes for all JSON strings
- Don't add any text after the closing }
- challengeText is REQUIRED
- All other fields are optional but recommended
